# Intro

We have most of the chat from the `#alttp` channel on the old SRL IRC logged from December 2012 up until November 2016. By that point, the active community had moved discussion of the game to the ALttP discord server (created in January 2016).

This is an attempt to distill and abridge these discussions into an intelligible format that doesn't take 8 years to read. Hopefully it offers a nice timeline of discoveries and knowledge about the game over that 3-4 year period. I'll also include bits and pieces related to how speedrun "culture" has changed over the years because I just find it interesting!

## SRL IRC?

Yes. SpeedRunsLive, an old site primarily oriented around races of speedgames, has an IRC chat server. There are some games that have divergent branches of history (ie. GoldenEye), but at this point in time, most real time speedrun communities congregate on this server to discuss their games (strats, glitches, routes, etc.). The most popular games have channels dedicated to discussions of that game (ie. the #alttp channel).

## A different time

The speedrun community likes to project an image of itself as less toxic than other gaming spaces. In 2012 though, that attitude has yet to completely form. If you read through these logs yourself, you'll see uses of the n-word, f\*g, r\*tard, "EG means Extra Gay" (and just general use of "gay" in a disparaging context), spergy/sperglord, tr\*nny, and on and on. Some of these happen quite frequently.

This was all just as wrong then as it is now. But it was also normalized Internet chat behavior at this time. I'm not going to bring too much of this stuff into this documentation, but if you peruse the logs yourself, be prepared. Especially when reading conversations that happen in the earlier parts of the logs.

## The beginning context

Krystal is working on "godtas2", a theory TAS of the Any% NMG run. At the time, this category is simply called Any% No S&Q, with an implicit ban on EG, mirror DMD, and TR skip (kind of the only major glitches they know). It also of course has an implicit ban on overworld deathwarping.

Tompa and Yuzuhara are working on a new TAS to update the 2009 TompaTAS. It uses an All Dungeons No EG ruleset.

### Known tech

#### Major glitches

This is the easy one. They barely know anything.
- They know EG. And even with this, they have a limited knowledge of how/where to arm it, trigger it, clip to it, EG strength, etc. etc.
- They know Mirror DMD, which involves some mirror shenanigans at the broken bridge on Death Mountain that ultimately allows you to jump off the mountain and access Dark World early. This is banned in the main category (which will one day be known as Any% NMG, though at the time, they don't call it that or consider it on those terms exactly).
- They know TR Mirror skip, which is where you place a portal part way through a ledge hop and are able to jump to Mirror Shield ledge early. Again, this is banned in the main category. They also know how to do it with hookdashing.
- They know fluteless Mire using the Mirror.
- Various things with up+down/left+right, which are being used in the TAS community, but the RTA community is mostly ignoring this stuff. One example is Ganonpot, which is only accessible with udlr as far as they know.
- They know the ~2m50s Any% route (found by FFGamer86 at some recent time in the game's history). The community mostly jokes about how dumb it is lol. Even more than we do now probably.
- They know about Green Tunic smithy (and accompanying fake world mechanics)
