I will say something that's changed now compared to this era is the speedrun community's relationship to things like Internet-fame, money, any kind of Twitch "meta" around increasing subs/viewers/whatever, etc. All of it is kind of viciously looked down on in 2013. To the speedrun community then, you're a sellout if you participate in such things. And now of course, people are indifferent. Most runners have alerts and incentives. Most serious runners have affiliate or partner. Participating in the game of building your stream, even if it means coming across as less "authentic", isn't looked at so negatively. It kind of reminds me of the "poptimism" change in music journalism (perhaps coincidentally but maybe not: occurred around the same time!).
