### Misc lingo differences

It's fun to note a number of differences in the community's language around certain things. Some I've noticed in this general vicinity:
- altipuh (instead of alttp, an artifact of the way people jokingly pronounce the game's abbreviation, but it also seeps into the way they type it)
- bash (instead of bonk)
- black knight (instead of ball n chain)
- Blind's dungeon, or simplified to Blind's (instead of Thieves' or Thieves' Town)
- butter sword (instead of gold sword)
- cast (instead of stream, as in 'broadcast')
- drill (instead of practice)
- firebird (instead of firebats re: Ganon fight)
- floating (instead of hovering)
- gator (instead of zazak)
- marathon (instead of gauntlet)
- pathing (instead of movement)
- torchspin (instead of capespin)
- wave (instead of cycle, ie: 2 wave lanmolas)
- wur (instead of WR)

Known tech that the community doesn't have a dedicated name for yet:
- dash turn
- nudging
